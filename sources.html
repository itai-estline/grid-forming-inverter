Reading the source tags

Short tags like V13, D26 appear next to numbers across the site. The V is the model version: V13 is the final model, built up from V1 over thirteen versions. The D is the entry in the decision log: D26 is decision 26, which records the two-inverter load-sharing result. A tag like Manual V11 points to the section of the extensions manual for that version. A number with no tag from my project comes from an outside source and has a footnote.
